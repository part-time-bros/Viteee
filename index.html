Physics P1 — Electrostatics & Current Electricity

P1·Q1ANS: D2021

Answer: Zero

A dipole = equal & opposite charges → net charge = 0. For 8 dipoles, net enclosed charge = 0.
By Gauss's Law: \(\Phi=Q_{\text{enc}}/\varepsilon_0 = 0\)
P1·Q2ANS: B2021

Answer: 7.2×10⁻⁷ J

\(W=q\Delta V = 2\times10^{-8}\times36 = 7.2\times10^{-7}\text{ J}\)
P1·Q3ANS: B2021

Answer: 4500 V

5 cm < R = 10 cm → inside the sphere. E = 0 inside, so potential = surface potential:
\(V=\dfrac{kQ}{R}=\dfrac{9\times10^9\times5\times10^{-8}}{0.1}=4500\text{ V}\)
P1·Q4ANS: C2021

Answer: 12000 N

At midpoint, r = 3 mm from each charge. Force from +2μC (repulsion) and −2μC (attraction) both push/pull the +3μC in the SAME direction.
\(F_1=F_2=\dfrac{9\times10^9\times2\times10^{-6}\times3\times10^{-6}}{(3\times10^{-3})^2}=6000\text{ N}\)
Net = 6000+6000 = 12000 N
P1·Q5ANS: A2019

Answer: C₁C₂(V₁−V₂)²/[2(C₁+C₂)]

Common potential after connection: \(V=\dfrac{C_1V_1+C_2V_2}{C_1+C_2}\)
\(\Delta U=U_i-U_f=\dfrac{C_1C_2(V_1-V_2)^2}{2(C_1+C_2)}\) ✓ (standard derivation)
P1·Q6ANS: B2018

Answer: 2 Ω

Balance condition: P/Q = R/S → S = QR/P = (5×4)/10 = 2 Ω
P1·Q7ANS: B2017

Answer: R (unchanged)

\(R=\rho L/A\). New: \(R'=\rho(2L)/(2A)=\rho L/A=R\). Doubling both L and A leaves R unchanged.
P1·Q8ANS: A2022

Answer: E²R/(R+r)²

\(I=E/(R+r)\) → \(P=I^2R=E^2R/(R+r)^2\)
P1·Q9ANS: C2015

Answer: EMF = E, r_eff = r/2

For n identical cells in parallel: EMF unchanged = E, internal resistance halved = r/n = r/2.
P1·Q10ANS: C2014

Answer: 1.7 Ω

\(R=\rho L/A=\dfrac{1.7\times10^{-8}\times1}{0.01\times10^{-6}}=\dfrac{1.7\times10^{-8}}{10^{-8}}=1.7\,\Omega\)
